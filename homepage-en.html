The famous game playable on the web!

What if I told you that playing Tic-Tac-Toe on paper is a thing of the past? With this website, I can assure you that you'll abandon the paper version 👀. All you need is next to nothing: a screen, a quiet place, a simple internet connection, and that's it. No more having to pull out a sheet of paper, a pencil, or a pen to play, and then, once your sheet is filled with twenty or so grids, having to find a space on it. And if you can't find one, having to search for another blank sheet to scribble on, and start all over again, until it never ends… poor trees that had to endure all those pencil and pen strokes 😔.

The different game modes

Of course, there will be different game modes available, but before we dive into the details, ALL game modes share the same rule: a maximum of two players must place the symbols (X and O) in a grid. The first player to align their identical symbols wins the game: a simple game with simple rules… KABOOM!!! 🤯. Furthermore, this database allows for several modes: a classic version (which corresponds to Player VS Player mode), a mode against an AI, a 5x5 mode (which is FINALLY available), etc. In short, plenty of ideas that could spring from the mind of a web developer's. With all that, if you say you're bored, I don't know what else to do…

It's time to start a game!

Here we are, it's time to get down to business and dive into the heart of the matter! 😎 Just below, you'll find a button that takes you to a catalog of available game modes, or you can find it in the navigation bar if you're on another page. And all of this is directly in your browser, no need to install any useless or dodgy applications, trust me 😉!.
