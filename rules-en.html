Small introduction

You are here because you have forgotten how to play a particular mode, isn’t it 😏? Don’t worry, it happens to everyone. You have detailed explanations of all the variants of the Morpion, even its classic version, we never know 👀 ! Take the time to read carefully to fully understand their own specificities, and then you will be at peace when you play.

Rules of Classic Tic-Tac-Toe

The classic Tic-Tac-Toe, or Player vs Player, is played on a 3x3 classic grid. The choice of symbols X and O is done by drawing lots, no need to create a war for a single symbol 😆. The goal for both players is to align three of their symbols in three possible ways:

To win? As shown just before, line them up first before the second player. If all the squares of the grid are filled without any player having managed to align their identical symbols, the game ends in a draw. Simple, isn’t it?

Rules of Tic-Tac-Toe VS IA

This game mode is identical to the classic Morpion, with ✨an extra little trick✨. It's always about playing in a grid of the same size as the classic version, and aligning its symbols in three different ways that you will find by clicking just here. But the second player is not your friend or a member of your family; it’s against the AI 😬. The choice of symbols? It will always be done at random and the AI will automatically choose the other symbol. ⚠️ Be careful, the AI will always play first if it chooses ONLY the symbol X, but the goal of the game remains the same, no surprise effect, promise 😉. AI is REALLY formidable and very hard to beat, so be strategic and good luck!

Rules of Tic-Tac-Toe 5x5

So this one, we’re onto a very big (and tall) Morpion. The goal is to align your symbols in three different ways, as usual you know the drill. But this time, the grid in question is not a simple 3x3 grid, but a 5x5 grid! , hence the name Morpion XL (oh my god 🫢).

Don't worry, it's not that complicated. You have the choice of one of two modes: against a player or against the AI itself. If you choose against AI, the same principle of drawing lots as in fashion against an AI. If you take the mode against a player, the same principle of drawing lots as in classic mode. And the rosters? All identical, except that here it's 5 rows of 5 columns to align, not 3 as in the classic mode.
